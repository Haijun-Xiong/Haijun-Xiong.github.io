About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Electronic Information and Communications (EIC) at Huazhong University of Science & Technology (HUST). I received my B.Sc. in Electronic Information Engineering from HUST in 2022.

My research interests broadly span Computer Vision and Deep Learning, including MLLM-based video understanding, Gait recognition, Human-centered vision, Fine-grained recognition, and Representation learning.
